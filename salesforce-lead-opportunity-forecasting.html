Fresh Start in Education

Salesforce Lead Opportunity Forecasting and Automated Closure

Sales needed a way to work an opportunity before converting the Lead. I built a guided process that estimates value, keeps each attempt and closes inactive work after 21 days.

Salesforce & CRM Automation Data & Analytics Commercial Strategy Operations
Project overview

A Screen Flow guides the user through four stages on the Lead record. Twelve months of purchase history supplies a forecast value, a custom object stores every attempt and a scheduled Flow clears out work that has gone quiet.

Key result Used 12 months of purchasing data and automatically closed inactive opportunities after 21 days.

01

The commercial problem

The standard Lead record had no good place to progress, value and close an opportunity before conversion. The same Lead might go through several successful or unsuccessful attempts, but showing all of them on the main record would make it difficult to use.

Sales also needed consistent data on where an opportunity came from, how it was handled and what happened. Inactive attempts could not be left open forever.

  • No structured pre-conversion opportunity process on the Lead record
  • Leads could carry several successful or unsuccessful attempts over time
  • Risk of overcrowding the Lead record with historical detail
  • No consistent way to prioritise higher-value activity

02

A guided, action-led Screen Flow

Once the Lead contains enough permitted data, a Salesforce action starts the Screen Flow. It marks the attempt as active and asks four questions: how the Lead was identified, how the team reached out, how the person responded and what the outcome was.

Each answer unlocks the next picklist. Users follow one sequence and the resulting data can be compared in reports.

  • Lead-readiness requirements before launch
  • Hidden activation checkbox
  • Identification, outreach, response and outcome picklists
  • Sequential unlocking of each stage

03

Forecast value from historical purchasing

At launch, Salesforce calculates a forecast from the previous 12 months of purchases. It checks organisation type, average rate, weekly hours and service length. MAT context can change the rate assumption for the same kind of organisation.

The value helps sales choose where to focus. It is a prioritisation signal based on past purchases, not a promise of future income.

  • 12 months of historical purchasing data
  • Organisation type, rate, hours and service duration
  • MAT-adjusted rate assumptions
  • Sales prioritisation by forecast value

04

Preserving every opportunity attempt

I created a custom object for the history. Every new cycle creates a new record, which updates as the user completes or changes the progression fields. Successful and failed attempts both remain available.

The Lead itself shows only the current cycle and a link to the latest related attempt. When a new cycle starts, the working fields reset without touching the earlier records.

  • Dedicated custom object per opportunity attempt
  • Dynamic record updates as fields changed
  • Current-only view on the Lead record
  • Lead-field purge and refresh between cycles

05

Automated closure of inactive opportunities

A timestamp changes whenever one of the four picklists changes. Scheduled Flow checks that date and closes any active attempt with no update for 21 days.

The automatic close uses its own outcome options. Reports can therefore distinguish an outcome entered by sales from one created by the inactivity rule.

  • Last-updated timestamp on progression change
  • Scheduled Flow monitoring inactivity
  • 21-day automated closure and deactivation
  • User-recorded vs. automated outcome distinction

06

Reportable history and sales feedback

The custom object turns repeated attempts into a reportable history, including the ones that went nowhere. Sales can review source, outreach, response, forecast value and outcome for every cycle.

That history gives the team evidence for changing its approach and removes some of the reliance on memory or scattered notes.

  • Full history of successful and unsuccessful attempts
  • Compiled identification, outreach and response data
  • Forecast-value and outcome reporting
  • Structured feedback for sales-process improvement

Outcomes

What sales gained

Consistent, measurable progression

Four dependent picklists record how each Lead was found, contacted and resolved.

Revenue-informed prioritisation

Twelve months of purchase history and MAT context give the team a comparable forecast value.

Clean records, full history

The custom object keeps every attempt while the Lead record stays focused on current work.

No opportunities left open indefinitely
