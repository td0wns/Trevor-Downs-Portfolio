Fresh Start in Education

AI-Ready Salesforce, Pardot and Website-Automation Architecture

I prepared Salesforce, Pardot/MCAE and the website for a future AI integration. The data and automations went live. The proposed AI layer did not.

AI-Assisted Innovation Salesforce & CRM Marketing Automation Data & Analytics
Project overview

Lead and Contact models, Campaign links, journey rules and Zapier handoffs give the organisation usable foundations now and a defined place for a future agent. Funding for AI tokens was withdrawn before any model or agent was added.

Key result The data, Campaign, segmentation and website automations are live. No production AI agent was deployed.
Capabilities
  • AI-ready data architecture
  • Salesforce data modelling
  • Campaign-based segmentation
  • Website-to-Pardot automation
  • Future-state AI use-case design
Platforms
  • Salesforce and Salesforce Flow
  • Salesforce Campaigns
  • Pardot / MCAE
  • Zapier
  • Website forms and integrations

01

The future-integration requirement

AI was a future objective during the Salesforce conversion. Before an agent could be useful, the CRM, Campaigns, website and marketing automation needed information it could read consistently.

Leads and Contacts needed clear segmentation, relationship and journey fields. Website activity needed a dependable route into MCAE. Any later AI decision also had to respect the existing consent, suppression and subscription rules.

  • Governed data required for reliable agent use
  • Structured Lead and Contact context
  • Clear journey-eligibility pathways
  • Reliable website-to-automation handoffs
  • Preservation of audience-governance rules

02

Agent-operable CRM data models

I designed Lead and Contact models with defined commercial, segmentation and relationship fields. A future agent could assess those fields instead of trying to infer everything from free text.

Campaign membership, engagement and journey eligibility sit alongside the record data. The same structure already works for ordinary Salesforce and MCAE processes without AI.

  • Structured Lead data model
  • Structured Contact data model
  • Governed commercial and relationship fields
  • Engagement and segmentation context
  • Reusable operational data foundations

03

Campaign segmentation and journey routing

I linked Salesforce Campaigns with Pardot/MCAE and defined how Leads and Contacts qualify for an email journey. Marketing can use Campaign, engagement and segmentation information in one decision.

That routing can support an agent later. For now, it already places suitable records into automated journeys under normal rules.

  • Salesforce Campaign connections
  • Lead and Contact segmentation pathways
  • Email-journey eligibility structures
  • Pardot/MCAE routing architecture
  • Sales and marketing alignment

04

Implemented website and Zapier automation

I used Zapier to pass website activity and form submissions into Pardot/MCAE. The handoff places prospect information into the relevant marketing workflow and keeps it tied to Salesforce records.

These integrations are live. Their usefulness does not depend on the unfunded AI work going ahead.

  • Website activity handoffs
  • Website form integrations
  • Zapier workflow orchestration
  • Pardot/MCAE automation entry
  • Connected digital prospect context

05

Planned agentic attribution enhancement

I specified an attribution enhancement in which an agent would review summaries of records, activities and interactions. That qualitative history would add context to the existing model.

The existing scores, weights, Campaigns and Account rules would remain visible. The AI was meant to add interpretation, not hide or replace the calculation.

  • Record-summary interpretation concept
  • Activity and interaction-history summaries
  • Qualitative attribution input
  • Transparent rules retained as the foundation
  • Governed future-state AI design

06

Completed foundations, undeployed AI layer

I completed the Salesforce Campaign, Pardot/MCAE, website, Zapier, segmentation and CRM data work in the agreed specification. It remains useful for journey routing and marketing automation on its own.

Funding for the required tokens was withdrawn before integration. No production agent, generative-AI decision process or AI-driven attribution layer was deployed.

  • Completed data and Campaign architecture
  • Completed segmentation and journey pathways
  • Completed website and Zapier handoffs
  • Operational value without AI dependency
  • Clear boundary around undeployed functionality

Outcomes
