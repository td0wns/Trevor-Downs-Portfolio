Fresh Start in Education

AI-Ready Salesforce, Pardot and Website-Automation Architecture

Designed and built governed Salesforce, Pardot/MCAE, Campaign, website and automation foundations to an agreed AI-ready specification, creating structured commercial context and implemented website handoffs while keeping planned AI functionality clearly separate from what was deployed.

AI-Assisted Innovation Salesforce & CRM Marketing Automation Data & Analytics
Project overview

Created structured Lead and Contact data models, Salesforce Campaign connections, Pardot/MCAE segmentation and journey pathways, plus Zapier handoffs from website activity and forms. These operational foundations were completed for future agent integration; the planned AI execution and agentic-attribution layers were not deployed after token funding was withdrawn.

Key result Completed the governed data, Campaign, segmentation and website-automation foundations required by the AI-ready specification; no production AI agent was deployed.

01

The future-integration requirement

AI integration was presented as a future objective during the Salesforce conversion. Before an agent could operate reliably, the underlying CRM, Campaign, website and marketing-automation systems needed structured, governed information rather than dependence on disconnected or unstructured context.

Leads and Contacts required clear segmentation, relationship and email-journey eligibility information. Website activity also needed to reach Pardot/MCAE workflows consistently, and any future AI decision layer would need to preserve consent, suppression, subscription and audience-governance rules.

  • Governed data required for reliable agent use
  • Structured Lead and Contact context
  • Clear journey-eligibility pathways
  • Reliable website-to-automation handoffs
  • Preservation of audience-governance rules

02

Agent-operable CRM data models

Designed structured Salesforce Lead and Contact models intended to make relevant commercial, segmentation and relationship context available through governed fields and relationships. Future agents could therefore assess records using defined operational data rather than relying only on free-text interpretation.

The architecture connected record context with Campaign membership, engagement information and journey eligibility while keeping the data useful to conventional Salesforce and marketing-automation processes.

  • Structured Lead data model
  • Structured Contact data model
  • Governed commercial and relationship fields
  • Engagement and segmentation context
  • Reusable operational data foundations

03

Campaign segmentation and journey routing

Connected Salesforce Campaigns with Pardot/MCAE so Lead, Contact, engagement and segmentation context could inform marketing processes. Defined pathways through which suitable records could be assessed for relevant email journeys.

The routing design provided the structure a future agent would need to interpret eligibility, while immediately supporting governed segmentation and conventional automated journey placement without AI.

  • Salesforce Campaign connections
  • Lead and Contact segmentation pathways
  • Email-journey eligibility structures
  • Pardot/MCAE routing architecture
  • Sales and marketing alignment

04

Implemented website and Zapier automation

Used Zapier to connect website activity and form submissions with Pardot/MCAE automations. The handoffs transferred relevant prospect information into marketing workflows and connected website behaviour with the wider Salesforce Campaign, Lead and Contact architecture.

Unlike the future AI layer, these website-to-Pardot integrations were implemented functionality and remained operationally useful regardless of whether agent funding proceeded.

  • Website activity handoffs
  • Website form integrations
  • Zapier workflow orchestration
  • Pardot/MCAE automation entry
  • Connected digital prospect context

05

Planned agentic attribution enhancement

Designed a future attribution enhancement in which an AI agent would review summaries of CRM records, activities and interactions. Its purpose was to interpret qualitative interaction history as an additional signal alongside the existing attribution model.

The concept preserved transparent scoring, weighting, Campaign, Account and organisational context as the governed foundation. AI-supported interpretation was intended to complement those rules rather than replace or obscure them.

  • Record-summary interpretation concept
  • Activity and interaction-history summaries
  • Qualitative attribution input
  • Transparent rules retained as the foundation
  • Governed future-state AI design

06

Completed foundations, undeployed AI layer

Delivered the Salesforce Campaign, Pardot/MCAE, website, Zapier, segmentation and CRM-data components required by the agreed specification. These foundations retained value for structured journey routing and marketing automation even without an AI model.

Funding for the required AI tokens was withdrawn before an AI model or agent could be integrated. No production agent, generative-AI decision process or AI-driven attribution layer was deployed, and the case study does not claim otherwise.

  • Completed data and Campaign architecture
  • Completed segmentation and journey pathways
  • Completed website and Zapier handoffs
  • Operational value without AI dependency
  • Clear boundary around undeployed functionality
