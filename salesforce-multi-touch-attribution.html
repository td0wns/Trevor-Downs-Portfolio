Fresh Start in Education

Salesforce Multi-Touch Attribution and Revenue Reporting

I built this because a single person was rarely the whole buying story. The live Salesforce model weighs recent email, event and sales activity at both person and organisation level, then follows it through enquiries, referrals, opportunities and placements.

Salesforce & CRM Automation Data & Analytics Commercial Strategy Marketing
Project overview

Three Salesforce Flow routes handle Lead enquiries, Contact enquiries and Account-level referrals. The calculation looks back three weeks and checks Campaign activity, recognised domains, MAT and Account context, returning customers and the order of each touchpoint. Every result totals 100% and is saved for dashboards and revenue reports.

Key result Normalised applicable channel influence to 100% across events, mailshots, sales outreach and returning-customer relationships.

01

The commercial challenge

Education purchases often involved several people, and staff turnover was high. One person might open an email, another attend an event and a third submit the eventual enquiry. Role-based email addresses made the identity trail even less reliable.

First-touch and last-touch reports credited whichever individual happened to appear at one end of the journey. They missed the rest of the organisation's activity.

  • Large buying committees with several independently engaging stakeholders.
  • Enquiries or referrals frequently submitted by someone who had not personally engaged with earlier activity.
  • High staff turnover reducing the reliability of individual Lead and Contact records.
  • Frequent use of role-based email addresses across schools and local authorities.

02

Engagement tracking architecture

I used Salesforce Campaigns to record recent behaviour. MCAE added prospects after a qualifying email open or click. Scheduled sales calls used a separate Campaign, and event-kiosk records went into event-specific Campaigns.

A weekly Flow clears and rebuilds membership against a three-week window. Old activity therefore stops influencing the calculation once it is no longer recent.

  • MCAE mailshot engagement Campaigns
  • Sales-call engagement Campaigns
  • Event kiosk and event Campaigns
  • Weekly purge and repopulation Flow
  • Three-week rolling engagement window

03

Three attribution Flow paths

I created three entry points: a Lead enquiry, a Contact enquiry and an Account-level referral. Each one gathers current Campaign membership, MAT information, Account context and relevant Lead or Contact activity before it calculates a result.

The Account route checks the wider organisation. It loops through related contact data to find the most relevant domain instead of assuming the referrer acted alone.

  • Lead enquiry attribution Flow
  • Contact enquiry attribution Flow
  • Account-level referral attribution Flow
  • Related Lead and Contact engagement checks

04

Domain matching and organisational context

For non-government addresses, the model uses shared domains to find related Leads and Contacts. Custom Metadata Types hold the reference data. I excluded .gov.uk domains because sharing one does not reliably mean that two contacts belong to the same buying group.

Activity from several related people can strengthen a channel's score. The calculation also checks Account status, since an existing customer relationship can influence a later purchase.

  • Shared email-domain matching
  • .gov.uk domain exclusion
  • Custom Metadata Type domain reference data
  • MAT and Account-status weighting

05

Sequence-sensitive weighting and normalisation

Order matters. If a sales call came before a later mailshot interaction, for example, the call received more weight because it was more likely to have prompted the later activity. I also checked known purchasing patterns before assigning the final shares.

The model adds the channel scores, works out each proportional share and normalises the answer to 100% across events, customer history, mailshots and sales outreach.

  • Touchpoint-sequence weighting
  • Purchasing-pattern checks
  • Total-score calculation
  • Proportional normalisation to 100%

06

Attribution Model object and reporting

Salesforce stores each result in a custom Attribution Model object. Flow populates the channel shares and the relevant Lead, Contact, Accounts, Opportunity and placement links, so users do not have to assemble the record by hand.

Those records feed a sales dashboard and several revenue reports. Whenever a referral or purchase is recorded, teams get a current ROI snapshot they can use when deciding where to put sales and marketing effort.

  • Custom Attribution Model object
  • Lead, Contact and Account lookups
  • Opportunity and placement linkage
  • Sales dashboard and revenue reporting

Outcomes

What changed after launch

Buying-committee visibility

Reports now recognise activity across a buying group instead of crediting only the person who completed the final form.

Recent, relevant engagement

The weekly refresh removes activity after three weeks, so old engagement does not keep receiving credit indefinitely.

Safe organisational matching

Domain matching finds related people where the evidence is useful, while the .gov.uk exclusion avoids a known false match.

Operational resource allocation

Live results feed the dashboards and revenue reports used for current sales and marketing resource decisions.
