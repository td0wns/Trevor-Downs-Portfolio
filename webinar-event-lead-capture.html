Fresh Start in Education

Webinar and Event Registration, CRM and Follow-Up Automation

An event should leave more behind than a guest list. I set up the route from invitation to attendance and follow-up so participation remained visible in Salesforce and useful in MCAE.

Events & Engagement Marketing Automation Salesforce & CRM Data & Analytics
Project overview

I handled email activity for eight events and two webinars. Invitations, registrations, attendance and follow-up moved through MCAE and Salesforce Campaigns. I also operated or hosted the live Zoom sessions, but did not present the content.

Key result Supported email activity for eight events and two webinars, including invitations, promotion, CRM visibility, follow-up and live Zoom operation or hosting.

01

The disconnected event lifecycle

Promotion, registration, attendance and follow-up each produced useful information. Previously, those stages could behave like separate tasks instead of one participant history.

The new route had to respect permission, record participant status in Salesforce and send the right post-event message for what actually happened.

  • Several participant lifecycle stages
  • Need for CRM-visible engagement
  • Different invite and follow-up audiences
  • Event and webinar operational requirements
  • Participation as part of wider journeys

02

Invitation and promotion

I used MCAE's audience rules to choose eligible segments for each event. Emails covered the invitation, promotion and the practical details needed before registration or attendance.

The event's purpose determined the audience. The entire contact database was never the default answer.

  • Governed invitation audiences
  • Segmented promotional email activity
  • Event and webinar messaging
  • Registration calls to action
  • Permission-aware campaign execution

03

Registration and Salesforce Campaign membership

I represented registration through Salesforce Campaign membership. Each event or webinar has a shared CRM record, so the response is not trapped inside the marketing platform.

Form and registration activity sits beside the relevant Lead or Contact information. Teams can see who responded and how the event fits their wider relationship with the organisation.

  • Registration-form activity
  • Campaign Member representation
  • Lead and Contact visibility
  • Shared Salesforce event context
  • Marketing-to-sales information flow

04

Attendance capture and engagement visibility

Registration shows intent; attendance shows participation. I kept those statuses separate and visible through Campaign membership.

Attendance can then contribute to event-influence reporting alongside email and sales activity. It is one signal, not proof that the event alone caused a later sale.

  • Registration and attendance distinction
  • Event-specific engagement status
  • CRM-visible participation history
  • Event-influence reporting support
  • Connection with wider engagement context

05

Segmented post-event follow-up

I used participant status to choose the follow-up. A registrant, an attendee and somebody who did neither do not automatically receive the same message.

The post-event message can also feed a longer nurture, customer or buying-committee journey. The record remains useful after the call or venue closes.

  • Participation-based segmentation
  • Relevant attendee follow-up
  • Registration-status journeys
  • Ongoing nurture connections
  • Audience-specific communication

06

Webinar and cross-functional operations

I managed webinar invitations and marketing, then operated or hosted the Zoom call. My job was the technical running and participant experience, not presenting the material.

That required coordination with the people responsible for content, client relationships and later follow-up. Their responsibilities had to line up with the campaign and CRM timing.

  • Webinar invitation and promotion
  • Live Zoom operation or hosting
  • Participant experience support
  • Stakeholder coordination
  • Clear operational ownership boundary
