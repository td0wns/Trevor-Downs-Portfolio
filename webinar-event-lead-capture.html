Fresh Start in Education

Webinar and Event Registration, CRM and Follow-Up Automation

Connected event and webinar promotion, registration, attendance and follow-up through Salesforce Campaigns and MCAE audience journeys, giving participation a structured place within CRM visibility, segmented communication and wider customer engagement.

01

The disconnected event lifecycle

Event and webinar activity needed to operate as part of a wider commercial and engagement journey rather than as isolated invitations. Promotion, registration, attendance and follow-up each created useful information, but that value depended on the stages being visible and connected across marketing automation and CRM.

The operating model therefore needed to combine audience selection, permission-aware email activity, participant status, Salesforce visibility and relevant post-event communication.

  • Several participant lifecycle stages
  • Need for CRM-visible engagement
  • Different invite and follow-up audiences
  • Event and webinar operational requirements
  • Participation as part of wider journeys

02

Invitation and promotion

Used the governed MCAE audience architecture to select relevant, permission-aware segments for event and webinar communications. Email activity supported invitations, promotion and the practical information participants needed before registration or attendance.

Campaign preparation connected audience logic with the relevant event purpose rather than treating the contact database as one undifferentiated mailing list.

  • Governed invitation audiences
  • Segmented promotional email activity
  • Event and webinar messaging
  • Registration calls to action
  • Permission-aware campaign execution

03

Registration and Salesforce Campaign membership

Structured registration information so participant activity could be represented through Salesforce Campaign membership. Campaign records created a shared CRM reference for the event or webinar and made engagement available beyond the marketing platform.

This connected form and registration activity with the relevant Lead or Contact context, improving visibility of who had responded and how participation related to other commercial and relationship information.

  • Registration-form activity
  • Campaign Member representation
  • Lead and Contact visibility
  • Shared Salesforce event context
  • Marketing-to-sales information flow

04

Attendance capture and engagement visibility

Attendance information distinguished registration intent from actual participation. Recorded event and webinar engagement could remain visible through Campaign membership and contribute to the wider understanding of a prospect or customer journey.

This structure also supported event-influence reporting without presenting attendance as the sole cause of a later commercial outcome. Participation became one governed engagement signal alongside other marketing and sales activity.

  • Registration and attendance distinction
  • Event-specific engagement status
  • CRM-visible participation history
  • Event-influence reporting support
  • Connection with wider engagement context

05

Segmented post-event follow-up

Used participation state to support relevant MCAE follow-up rather than sending the same communication to every invited person. Registrants, attendees and other relevant segments could move into appropriate post-event journeys.

Follow-up maintained connection between the immediate event experience and longer customer, nurture or buying-committee journeys, allowing engagement to remain useful after the live activity ended.

  • Participation-based segmentation
  • Relevant attendee follow-up
  • Registration-status journeys
  • Ongoing nurture connections
  • Audience-specific communication

06

Webinar and cross-functional operations

Managed webinar invitation and marketing activity and supported live Zoom operation or hosting. Responsibilities covered the practical running of the call and participant experience rather than serving as the content presenter.

Event and webinar work required coordination with stakeholders responsible for content, client relationships and follow-up, connecting technical delivery with campaign and CRM processes.

  • Webinar invitation and promotion
  • Live Zoom operation or hosting
  • Participant experience support
  • Stakeholder coordination
  • Clear operational ownership boundary
