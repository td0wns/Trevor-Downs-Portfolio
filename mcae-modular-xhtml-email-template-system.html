Fresh Start in Education

Modular MCAE XHTML Email Template System

Proposed, designed and delivered MCAE's standard modular email-production framework after the Mailchimp migration removed the organisation's previous drag-and-drop content blocks, combining commercially informed design, reusable XHTML, cross-client testing and user enablement.

Marketing Automation Salesforce & CRM Operations AI-Assisted Innovation
Project overview

Translated effective Mailchimp campaign patterns into approximately 13 reusable XHTML modules across 13 ready-to-use MCAE templates. The framework supported up to 48 calculated layout combinations, powered around 28 emails across approximately 24 campaigns after launch, and reduced typical production time from several days to approximately one day.

Key result Created approximately 13 reusable modules across 13 templates, supporting up to 48 combinations and reducing typical production from several days to approximately one day.

01

The production gap after migration

Moving email marketing from Mailchimp to Salesforce Marketing Cloud Account Engagement removed access to the simple drag-and-drop content blocks previously used to assemble branded campaigns. Comparable functionality now required coded components.

The replacement needed to remain responsive, reliable in security-conscious recipient environments, consistent with an evolving brand system and practical for users who did not routinely work with XHTML.

  • Loss of the previous content-block workflow
  • Need for repeatable branded layouts
  • Users without routine code access
  • Strict recipient security environments
  • Mobile and desktop compatibility requirements

02

Commercially informed design research

Reviewed the organisation's strongest Mailchimp campaigns, using revenue won as the primary indicator of success. Identified recurring layouts, content structures and visual approaches associated with commercially effective campaigns and used those findings to sketch the new modular system.

Effective elements were adapted to the organisation's branding and evolving brand voice. The work formalised an existing informal voice and identified two additional voices that could support the wider persona-marketing approach.

  • Revenue-led Mailchimp campaign review
  • Layout and content-pattern analysis
  • Initial modular template sketches
  • Brand-system application
  • Persona-supporting creative direction

03

Reusable module architecture

Designed a library of approximately 13 modular XHTML snippets stored within 13 ready-to-use MCAE templates. Modules included hero banners, text blocks, calls to action, image panels, split image-and-text table layouts and footers.

Full-width and split-layout imagery could be positioned at the top, middle or bottom of an email. The resulting structure supported up to 48 calculated combinations, with approximately 20 to 30 configurations used in practice, without requiring a separate template for every campaign.

  • Hero-banner and text modules
  • Call-to-action and image-panel modules
  • Split image-and-text table layouts
  • Reusable footer structures
  • Up to 48 calculated combinations

04

Directed AI-assisted XHTML development

Defined each module's layout, structure, intended behaviour, branding requirements and content flexibility before directing AI-assisted XHTML creation through Gemini and Claude Code.

Ownership extended through manual code review and interpretation, visual inspection, test sends, behavioural validation, implementation and later AI-assisted debugging. This approach used generative tools to accelerate development while retaining human control over requirements, quality and deployment; it does not imply unaided authorship of all complex XHTML.

  • Detailed module specifications
  • Structured AI development direction
  • Manual XHTML review and interpretation
  • Visual and behavioural validation
  • AI-assisted debugging and compatibility work

05

Compatibility and security-conscious design

Validated responsive behaviour through MCAE's editor, repeated test sends and practical review in professional Gmail and Outlook inboxes. Checks covered layout integrity, text, images, buttons, links and behaviour across mobile and desktop.

Simple, dependable XHTML and minimal dependencies reduced the likelihood of unusual behaviour being blocked by recipient security controls. Images remained cosmetic rather than carrying essential meaning, while important information, links and calls to action remained available as live content if images failed or were disabled.

  • MCAE editor and repeated test sends
  • Gmail and Outlook rendering validation
  • Mobile and desktop review
  • Minimal dependency design
  • Live-text, image-independent actions

06

Standardised production and user enablement

Uploaded and organised hero banners, logos, approved imagery and wider brand assets for consistent reuse. Prepared modules allowed routine users to combine, reorder and replace content without editing the underlying XHTML, while preserving approved layouts.

Produced written instructions and trained line managers to select templates, manage images and adapt campaigns. The framework became the organisation's standard and only marketing-email template system, supported around 28 emails across approximately 24 campaigns after launch, and reduced typical production time to approximately one day.

  • Reusable brand-asset library
  • Drag-and-drop module assembly
  • Written user instructions
  • Line-manager training
  • Incremental template-library expansion

Outcomes

What the modular framework delivered

Flexible reuse at scale

Approximately 13 modules across 13 MCAE templates provided up to 48 calculated layout combinations without an unmanageable template library.

Faster production

Pre-approved structures reduced repeated design and review cycles, taking typical email production from several days to approximately one day.

Reliable rendering

Simple XHTML, live text and repeated testing supported dependable behaviour across MCAE, Gmail, Outlook, mobile and desktop.

Operational adoption

The system supported around 28 emails across approximately 24 campaigns and became the organisation's standard marketing-email production framework.
