Hillsong

Large-Scale International Conference Operations and Volunteer Leadership

Managed operational delivery and supported planning for large, multi-day international conferences across venue flow, access systems, volunteer leadership, participant services, VIP coordination, risk controls and rapid live issue resolution.

Events & Engagement Operations Leadership & Stakeholder Management
Project overview

Coordinated operations across five week-long conferences with more than 30,000 attendees each, five smaller conferences of approximately 12,000 attendees each and a specialised creativity conference. Combined detailed planning, venue logistics, volunteer deployment, access control, stakeholder coordination and contingency response across high-pressure live environments. Progressed from Operations Team Member in 2017 to Co-Manager in 2018 and the internally titled Event Operations Manager from 2019 to July 2021, all within an education-linked internship.

Key result Managed operations for conferences of 30,000+ attendees, with peak oversight of approximately 450 people through team leaders.

01

Conference scale and complexity

Major conferences ran for a week and brought more than 30,000 attendees into complex Sydney venues. Operations spanned main sessions, masterclasses, tradeshows, concession stands, VIP areas, security checkpoints and participant-facing services, often running concurrently.

The wider programme also included five smaller conferences of approximately 12,000 attendees each and a specialist creativity and musicians conference, requiring consistent operational standards across different formats and scales.

  • Five week-long conferences of 30,000+ attendees
  • Five conferences of approximately 12,000 attendees
  • Several concurrent programme and venue areas
  • High-volume participant movement
  • International audiences and teams

02

Detailed planning and delivery control

Used detailed, time-sensitive operational plans and Waterfall delivery methods to coordinate setup, logistics, programme dependencies, administrative work and on-site execution. Planning supported schedule, budget, quality and attendee-experience expectations.

Each operational workstream required clear sequencing and handoffs so venue teams, volunteers, security, participant services and programme areas could act together during live delivery.

  • Time-sensitive operational plans
  • Waterfall delivery methodology
  • Venue setup and logistics
  • Cross-functional handoffs
  • On-time and on-budget delivery support

03

Venue flow, checkpoints and access systems

Designed and implemented event flows for attendees, volunteers, staff and VIPs across internal and external venue spaces. Multi-tiered access systems and security checkpoints helped control movement between public, operational and restricted areas.

Created repeatable protocols and trained teams to execute them, supporting clearer movement and more consistent delivery across future conferences as well as the immediate live event.

  • Attendee and staff movement plans
  • Security checkpoint coordination
  • Multi-tier volunteer and staff access
  • VIP-area operational controls
  • Repeatable venue-flow protocols

04

Volunteer leadership at scale

Led and coordinated cross-functional teams across logistics, operations and client-facing work. Managed approximately six regular direct reports and worked with around 20 changing reporting relationships during conferences.

As part of the conference lead's right-hand team, held broad delegated authority across most operational areas and exercised peak oversight of approximately 450 people through their team leaders. This was not a conventional 450-person direct-report structure.

Training, coaching, role clarity and leadership development helped integrate volunteers from diverse international backgrounds and built capability that could be reused beyond one conference.

  • Approximately six regular direct reports
  • Around 20 changing conference reports
  • Peak oversight of 450 through team leaders
  • Briefing, allocation, training and coaching
  • Development of future team leaders

05

Participant, VIP and stakeholder operations

Coordinated participant-facing services and VIP operations with security, audiovisual, internal department and event teams. Stakeholder liaison connected guest requirements with the practical realities of venue access, scheduling and live programme delivery.

Work also included coordination with NGO partners for live events and support for fundraising and community-engagement objectives, requiring clear communication across organisations as well as internal teams.

  • Participant-facing operational support
  • VIP guest coordination
  • Security and audiovisual liaison
  • NGO partnership delivery
  • Client and stakeholder communication

06

Live risk, contingency and improvement

High-volume live events required rapid troubleshooting, conflict de-escalation and contingency responses while maintaining participant experience and operational control. Prepared risk and contingency plans provided a basis for acting when live conditions changed.

On-site learning, post-event support and evaluation informed future processes. Effective contingency implementation during live events contributed to progression into Event Operations Manager.

  • Risk and contingency planning
  • Rapid live issue resolution
  • Conflict de-escalation
  • Post-event evaluation
  • Continuous operational improvement
