Hillsong

Large-Scale International Conference Operations and Volunteer Leadership

I progressed from an operations team member to leadership roles on week-long international conferences with more than 30,000 attendees.

Events & Engagement Operations Leadership & Stakeholder Management
Project overview

The programme included five 30,000+ week-long conferences, five events of about 12,000 and a specialist creativity conference. During the internship I moved from Operations Team Member in 2017 to Co-Manager in 2018 and the internally titled Event Operations Manager from 2019 to July 2021.

Key result Managed operations for conferences of 30,000+ attendees, with peak oversight of approximately 450 people through team leaders.
Capabilities
  • Large-scale event operations
  • Volunteer and team leadership
  • Venue flow and access control
  • Risk and contingency management
  • VIP and stakeholder coordination
Operational systems
  • Event-management systems
  • Registration processes
  • Scheduling tools
  • Multi-tier access controls
  • Venue and checkpoint protocols

01

Conference scale and complexity

The major conferences ran for a week in large Sydney venues. More than 30,000 people moved between main sessions, masterclasses, tradeshows, concessions, VIP areas and security checkpoints, often at the same time.

Five events of about 12,000 and a specialist conference for creatives and musicians added different formats to the same programme.

  • Five week-long conferences of 30,000+ attendees
  • Five conferences of approximately 12,000 attendees
  • Several concurrent programme and venue areas
  • High-volume participant movement
  • International audiences and teams

02

Detailed planning and delivery control

I worked from detailed, time-sensitive plans and a Waterfall delivery approach. Setup, logistics, administration and programme dependencies all had to arrive at the venue in the right order.

The handoffs mattered. Venue teams, volunteers, security and participant services needed to know what changed, when it changed and who acted next.

  • Time-sensitive operational plans
  • Waterfall delivery methodology
  • Venue setup and logistics
  • Cross-functional handoffs
  • On-time and on-budget delivery support

03

Venue flow, checkpoints and access systems

I planned movement for attendees, volunteers, staff and VIPs inside and outside the venues. Access levels and checkpoints controlled the boundary between public, working and restricted areas.

I documented the protocols and trained teams to run them. The same work could then be reused at the next conference instead of being reinvented on site.

  • Attendee and staff movement plans
  • Security checkpoint coordination
  • Multi-tier volunteer and staff access
  • VIP-area operational controls
  • Repeatable venue-flow protocols

04

Volunteer leadership at scale

I led teams in logistics, operations and participant-facing work. There were about six regular direct reports and roughly 20 changing reporting relationships during conferences.

As part of the conference lead's right-hand team, I had delegated authority across most operational areas. At peak, that meant oversight of about 450 people through their team leaders, not 450 direct reports.

Briefings, coaching and clear roles helped volunteers from different countries work together. I also developed people who could lead future teams.

  • Approximately six regular direct reports
  • Around 20 changing conference reports
  • Peak oversight of 450 through team leaders
  • Briefing, allocation, training and coaching
  • Development of future team leaders

05

Participant, VIP and stakeholder operations

I coordinated participant services and VIP operations with security, audiovisual and internal teams. Guest requirements still had to fit the realities of access, timing and the live programme.

I also worked with NGO partners on live events tied to fundraising and community activity. That added external organisations to an already busy communication chain.

  • Participant-facing operational support
  • VIP guest coordination
  • Security and audiovisual liaison
  • NGO partnership delivery
  • Client and stakeholder communication

06

Live risk, contingency and improvement

At this size, problems arrive in public and in real time. I handled rapid troubleshooting, conflict de-escalation and contingency decisions from plans prepared before doors opened.

After each event, on-site learning fed the next set of plans. Effective contingency work also contributed to my progression into Event Operations Manager.

  • Risk and contingency planning
  • Rapid live issue resolution
  • Conflict de-escalation
  • Post-event evaluation
  • Continuous operational improvement
