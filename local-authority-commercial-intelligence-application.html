Fresh Start in Education

Local-Authority Commercial Intelligence Application

Designed and built a custom Salesforce decision-support application that consolidated Local Authority relationships, Contracts, pricing, Accounts, governing organisations, Bids, Opportunities and Referrals into dynamic summaries, risk indicators and validated prioritisation scores used to guide pricing, bidding and relationship-management decisions.

AI-Assisted Innovation Salesforce & CRM Data & Analytics Commercial Strategy Automation
Project overview

Created a custom Salesforce app spanning 200+ Local Authorities, more than 1,000 related Accounts and around 2,500 Bids per year. Combined custom objects, AI-assisted Apex and visual components, Contract and pricing context, win/loss analysis, expiry warnings and a validated scoring model to give commercial teams one shared view for pricing, relationship value and resource prioritisation.

Key result Consolidated 200+ Local Authorities, 1,000+ related Accounts and around 2,500 annual Bids into one dynamic commercial-intelligence application.

01

The fragmented commercial picture

Local Authority information was distributed across Account, Contract, pricing, Bid, Opportunity, Referral and governing-organisation records. Users had to locate each item and manually assemble the wider relationship picture before they could make a commercial decision. Although a hierarchy existed, operational users could see individual one-to-one relationships but not the one-to-many view showing every client connected to the same Local Authority.

This made prioritisation difficult and created practical risk. Bespoke Contract pricing could be overlooked, sometimes contributing to Accounts being charged incorrectly, while no consolidated view existed for comparing Local Authority performance or Bid outcomes.

  • Siloed commercial and relationship data
  • Manual research across several objects
  • Risk of overlooked custom pricing
  • No consolidated Bid win/loss view
  • Limited basis for regional prioritisation

02

Custom objects and relationship model

Created custom Local Authority and Bid objects and connected them with Salesforce Accounts, Opportunities and the organisation's custom Contract object. Every Bid identified the Account to which it was submitted, allowing the relevant parent Local Authority to be populated and successful Bids to progress into Opportunities.

Parent-account validation rules required relevant organisation Accounts to use the correct Local Authority Account, protecting the relationships on which every downstream summary and score depended.

  • Custom Local Authority object
  • Custom Bid object and Account lookup
  • Opportunity progression for successful Bids
  • Contract-object integration
  • Parent-account hierarchy validation

03

Dynamic relationship and activity summaries

Used AI-assisted Apex to surface child Accounts, Master Report Accounts, recent Bid recipients, Bid totals, Referral totals, recent activity and win/loss information on each Local Authority record. Master Report Account summaries showed which Multi-Academy Trusts and other private governing organisations operated within an area.

The related-Account and Bid-list pattern was adapted from functionality observed in another Salesforce app already purchased by the organisation. Comparable logic was implemented for the custom Local Authority object, avoiding additional supplier development without claiming the underlying list concept as original.

  • Dynamic child-Account lists
  • Private governing-organisation summaries
  • Total and recent Bid and Referral views
  • Six-month Bid-recipient visibility
  • Consolidated Bid win/loss ratio

04

Contract, pricing and risk visibility

Pulled Contract start date, end date, agreement-to-bid status and bespoke service pricing into the Local Authority record. This gave users handling client enquiries a direct route to the correct agreed charge rate without manually opening and interpreting a separate Contract.

The application added warnings for Contracts due to expire within 60 days and separate indicators for already expired Contracts. Custom pricing remained available on the Local Authority record; the high-level summary card showed pricing status rather than the price itself.

  • Contract start and end summaries
  • Agreement-to-bid status
  • Accessible bespoke pricing records
  • 60-day expiry warnings
  • Expired-Contract indicators

05

Validated scoring and dynamic ranking

Designed a numerical model that compared Referral volume with the number of Accounts in each Local Authority area. Stronger Referral generation relative to Account coverage increased the score, weaker performance lowered it, and proportionate performance remained neutral.

Bid and Referral activity, connected Account volume and Contract status informed the wider prioritisation model. An active Contract added a bonus without becoming a universal requirement, because not every Local Authority relationship needed one. The logic was validated before being applied to the ranking.

Bid win/loss ratios also helped decide where limited bidding effort should go. In some Local Authorities, thousands of opportunities could be available in a month—far more than the organisation could submit—so relative conversion evidence mattered to resource allocation.

  • Referral-to-Account performance comparison
  • Bid and Referral activity signals
  • Connected Account influence
  • Active-Contract scoring bonus
  • Validated dynamic card ordering

06

AI-assisted interface and end-to-end ownership

Directed AI-assisted Apex and custom visual component development, including work with Codex, to create a high-level card interface. Crown, green-, yellow- and red-flag indicators communicated relative value alongside recent Referrals, recent Bids, win/loss ratio, Contract status, custom-pricing status and agreement-to-bid status.

Owned the project end to end through problem identification, requirements, data relationships, scoring logic, development direction, output review, testing, validation, configuration and deployment. The page does not imply unaided authorship of all Apex or visual code. The resulting application was adopted in production and refined as user needs emerged.

  • AI-assisted Apex and component development
  • Human-defined requirements and scoring logic
  • Manual review, testing and validation
  • Dynamic visual ranking and status icons
  • Production rollout and feedback-led refinement

Outcomes

What the intelligence application enabled

One commercial view

Replaced fragmented manual research with a shared view of Local Authority relationships, Contracts, pricing, Accounts, Bids, Referrals and Opportunities.

Current prioritisation

Scores, summaries and rankings refresh as source data changes, helping distinguish exceptionally valuable relationships from middling ones.

Reduced pricing risk

Made bespoke Contract pricing easier for users to find when responding to client enquiries, reducing reliance on siloed records and memory.

Reusable commercial evidence

Established Bid win/loss and relationship-value evidence that helps allocate limited effort where opportunity volumes exceed submission capacity.
