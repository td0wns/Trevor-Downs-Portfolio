Fresh Start in Education

MAT and Private Governing-Organisation Commercial Architecture

A school rarely buys in complete isolation. I built a Salesforce and MCAE model that keeps its MAT or governing organisation visible and uses purchases elsewhere in that group as a signal for marketing and sales.

Salesforce & CRM Automation Data & Analytics Commercial Strategy Marketing
Project overview

I mapped school Accounts to their governing organisations, built the Salesforce rules and won senior approval for the commercial model. Automation aligns Accounts, Contacts and Leads. A related purchase can then trigger MCAE checks, grading, nurture and sales handoff.

Key result Mapped approximately 50% of school Accounts to 215 MATs and private governing organisations.

01

The commercial problem

About half of the school Accounts belonged to a MAT, independent-school group or another private governing organisation. Every school also had a separate Local Authority context.

Treating each school as a lone buyer hid shared governance and purchases elsewhere in the group. Salesforce had no complete structure for those links, and an unconverted Lead could not use a standard Account lookup.

  • Approximately half of school accounts belonged to a private governing organisation
  • Risk of treating connected schools as isolated buying entities
  • No standard Lead-to-Account lookup before conversion
  • No process for acting on purchases made elsewhere in the same organisation

02

Mapping the governing-organisation landscape

I researched the governing membership of the full school database, including MATs, independent-school groups and other private bodies. Each Account was mapped to the right organisation or marked as having no known membership.

An additional parent relationship holds the private governing organisation. Schools can therefore retain that information and their Local Authority relationship at the same time.

  • Approximately 215 private governing organisations
  • Approximately 50% of school accounts mapped
  • Additional parent-account relationships
  • Dual local-authority and private-governance context

03

Lookup keys and metadata-driven alignment

Custom Metadata Types hold the assignment rules. Salesforce automation uses them to align Accounts, Contacts and Leads with the correct MAT or master Account.

I added lookup-key fields so unconverted Leads could keep their governing context despite the missing standard Account relationship. Validation rules catch incomplete or inaccurate assignments.

  • Custom Metadata Type-driven Flows
  • Custom lookup-key fields for Leads
  • Account, Contact and Lead alignment automation
  • Validation rules protecting hierarchy integrity

04

One dynamic, multi-object Screen Flow

I made one Screen Flow behave differently according to where it is opened. On a school Account it sets the private governing body. On a Local Authority or one of its departments, it blocks an inappropriate assignment.

On a Lead, users see the same interface while different fields are populated behind the scenes. On a MAT Account, the Flow lists related Accounts and Leads, helps users avoid similarly named MATs and allows them to request a new MAT record when needed.

  • School Account alignment path
  • Local-authority Account exclusion path
  • Lead-specific field population path
  • MAT Account related-record summary path
  • Final review before submission

05

Domain matching for new records

I documented thousands of email domains and stored them in Custom Metadata Types. When a new Lead or Contact has no governing link, Flow checks for an exact domain or an approved wildcard variation.

Government and personal domains are excluded because they would create false matches. If no valid domain is found, the Flow ends quietly and does not interrupt the user.

  • Thousands of documented organisational domains
  • Exact and wildcard domain matching
  • Government and personal domain exclusions
  • Quiet completion on no match

06

Commercial strategy, adoption and activation

The commercial premise was simple: a purchase by one organisation may be relevant to others in the same group. When an Account purchases, related non-purchasing Accounts and Leads can enter a Salesforce Campaign synchronised with MCAE.

I presented the model over several meetings and secured formal approval from the Director, then the CEO. It changed how the company viewed related Accounts and influenced email marketing and the wider brand voice.

MCAE checks whether related prospects are legitimate, adjusts their qualification, places suitable records into follow-up and sends qualified Leads or Accounts to the right sales team.

  • Purchase-triggered Salesforce Campaigns
  • Salesforce-to-MCAE Campaign synchronisation
  • MCAE legitimacy validation and grading
  • Automated qualified-record sales routing
