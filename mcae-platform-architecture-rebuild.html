Fresh Start in Education

Mailchimp-to-MCAE Migration, Data Remediation and Platform Rebuild

Led the transition from a high-risk Mailchimp and Pro-Net email and CRM environment into a newly built Salesforce Marketing Cloud Account Engagement instance, creating the governed foundations for scalable, permission-aware marketing automation.

Marketing Automation Salesforce & CRM Data & Analytics Operations
Project overview

Served as sole owner of the Mailchimp-to-MCAE migration and instance build from October 2024 to January 2026. Diagnosed and remediated the legacy database, migrated viable audiences while Salesforce was released in batches, and rebuilt consent, suppression, preference, unsubscribe and audience-governance infrastructure from the ground up.

Key result Reduced approximately 30,000 legacy contacts to 19,000 viable prospects and improved successful-send rate to approximately 99%.

01

The inherited environment

The organisation relied on Mailchimp manually synchronised with the legacy Pro-Net CRM. The environment contained approximately 30,000 contacts and carried substantial data-quality, sender-reputation, spam-placement, suppression, consent and engagement risks.

Mailchimp needed to be phased out without interrupting active marketing, while Salesforce was being introduced in batches across the organisation. The replacement platform therefore had to support transition work and establish stronger long-term governance at the same time.

  • Fragmented Mailchimp and Pro-Net processes
  • Poor-quality and disengaged legacy records
  • Weak suppression and consent controls
  • Damaged sender reputation and spam risk
  • A phased Salesforce rollout running in parallel

02

Database diagnosis and remediation

Audited the legacy contact base to distinguish viable prospects from records affected by hard bounces, unsubscribes, suppression requirements, disengagement or unreliable data. Records were corrected, suppressed, removed or sunsetted according to their condition and permission status.

Approximately 7,000 non-viable records were removed, including hard-bounced, unsubscribed and otherwise suppressed contacts. The wider remediation process reduced the original database to around 19,000 prospects suitable for the governed MCAE environment.

  • Legacy-data quality assessment
  • Hard-bounce and unsubscribe remediation
  • Suppression and disengagement management
  • Correction and sunsetting of unusable records
  • Selection of viable migration audiences

03

Migration and MCAE instance build

Planned and delivered the migration from Mailchimp into Salesforce Marketing Cloud Account Engagement while supporting the transition away from Pro-Net and the staged Salesforce rollout. This required maintaining operational continuity while the organisation's underlying systems were changing.

Built the MCAE instance from scratch, excluding one pre-existing form, and configured the core structures required for audience management, email operations and connection with the new Salesforce environment.

  • Mailchimp-to-MCAE migration planning
  • Pro-Net CRM transition support
  • Phased Salesforce rollout alignment
  • MCAE instance configuration
  • Operational continuity during migration

04

Permission-aware governance foundations

Created the organisation's suppression policy and consent-capture policy, then translated those rules into the operational foundations of the new platform. Unsubscribe handling and audience preference management were rebuilt to give prospect permission and suitability a clearer role in campaign decisions.

Dynamic lists, static lists, suppression lists, forms, preference pages and automation rules provided the building blocks for cleaner segmentation and repeatable audience management.

  • Suppression policy
  • Consent-capture policy
  • Unsubscribe governance
  • Audience preference management
  • List and automation-rule foundations

05

Send hygiene and operational recovery

Combined database remediation with stronger suppression, consent and audience controls to reduce the risks created by broken records, weak governance and poor list hygiene. The rebuild provided a more reliable basis for recurring email and marketing-automation work.

Successful-send rate improved to approximately 99%. This figure represents messages accepted for delivery and is not presented as a measure of inbox placement.

  • Email send-hygiene controls
  • Sender-reputation recovery activity
  • Permission-aware audience selection
  • Repeatable campaign foundations
  • Successful-send monitoring

Reduced operational risk
