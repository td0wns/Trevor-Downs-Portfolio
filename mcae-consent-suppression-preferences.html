Fresh Start in Education

Initial MCAE Audience, Consent and Suppression Architecture

Designed and implemented the self-healing audience foundation for a rebuilt MCAE instance, governing qualification, subscriptions, suppression, remediation and Salesforce-informed segmentation through January 2026.

Marketing Automation Salesforce & CRM Data & Analytics Operations Implementation & Change
Project overview

Built approximately 400 MCAE lists within a governed architecture spanning dynamic qualification, master suppression, subscription preferences, data remediation, segmentation, lifecycle management and buyer-readiness foundations. The system moved prospects between eligible, suppressed, remediation and requalified states as their data, permission and engagement changed.

Key result Created a self-healing audience system of approximately 400 MCAE lists supporting three commercial audiences and continuous prospect requalification.

01

The audience-management challenge

The new MCAE environment needed to do more than execute campaigns. It had to distinguish clean, sendable and permission-aware prospects from records that were unsubscribed, suppressed, disengaged, incomplete or awaiting remediation.

The organisation also served several commercial audiences with different qualification rules, buying relationships and lifecycle patterns. Static manual lists could not reliably keep audience eligibility, data quality and subscription state aligned as prospect records changed.

  • Multiple audiences with distinct commercial purposes
  • Changing permission and engagement states
  • Incomplete business and geographic data
  • Risk of broken merge tags in active sends
  • Need for continuous, automatic requalification

02

Dynamic qualification and list architecture

Structured approximately 400 MCAE lists around two different kinds of evidence. Dynamic lists represented statements that were currently true, such as whether a prospect had clean data, remained engaged or met an audience's minimum qualification rules. Static micro-segmentation lists preserved historical actions that had previously occurred.

Each core audience used dynamic qualifier lists requiring minimum viable prospect information, including a name, email address, relevant subscription type and exclusion from the Master Suppression List. As those inputs changed, audience membership adjusted automatically.

  • Dynamic lists for current prospect states
  • Static lists for historical actions
  • Minimum viable audience requirements
  • Automated entry and removal
  • Continuous prospect requalification

03

Suppression, remediation and data recovery

Created a centralised, fully dynamic Master Suppression List composed of issue-specific controls covering unsubscribes, do-not-email records, hard bounces, blank first names, duplicates, test records, messaging fatigue and other non-viable cases.

Suppression was designed as an operational routing mechanism rather than a permanent final destination. Records with correctable issues were removed from active audiences and routed into remediation. Dedicated misfit lists retained visibility of missing business-type or local-authority data without treating incomplete records as permanently invalid. Once corrected, prospects could qualify again automatically.

  • Dynamic Master Suppression List
  • Issue-specific suppression controls
  • Misfit and geographic-misfit lists
  • Data correction and enrichment workflows
  • Automatic return to eligible audiences

04

Consent and subscription governance

Built branded preference and unsubscribe pages with cascading logic that respected both audience-level choices and universal unsubscribe requirements. Prospects could leave an individual audience or channel without necessarily losing every appropriate subscription, while a universal unsubscribe removed them from all lists capable of sending email.

Enquiry and gated-content forms routed opt-ins into source-tracking lists, preserving the context in which each subscription was obtained. Most-recently-updated logic helped ensure that the latest subscription state governed activity across connected systems.

  • Branded preference and unsubscribe pages
  • Audience-level opt-out controls
  • Universal unsubscribe compliance
  • Subscription-source tracking
  • Latest-state subscription governance

05

Three-audience segmentation model

Structured MCAE around three commercial audiences: provision and services, a community newsletter supporting buying-committee influence, and professional development sold to schools. Cross-audience rules allowed prospects to belong to more than one audience where they met the relevant criteria without weakening qualification requirements.

Audience segmentation incorporated primary schools, secondary schools, academies and local authority teams. Salesforce-synchronised business-type and local-authority data, combined with formula-derived region logic, automated county and geographic segmentation and reduced manual list maintenance.

  • Provision and services audience
  • Community and buying-committee audience
  • Professional-development audience
  • Business-type and client-archetype logic
  • Salesforce-informed geographic segmentation

06

A production foundation for continued improvement

Completed the initial architecture in January 2026 as the production foundation for governed campaign operations across three commercial audiences. The build established the rules, controls and data routes needed for reliable qualification and later lifecycle development.

From February 2026, ongoing monitoring and optimisation became a separate phase of work. That phase extends the foundation through reversible qualification, predictive buyer grading, score and profile degradation, certainty-state routing and human review.

  • Completed initial architecture
  • Production handoff in January 2026
  • Three governed commercial audiences
  • Clear boundary between build and optimisation
  • Foundation for ongoing lifecycle governance

Outcomes

What the initial architecture enabled

Continuously governed audiences

Dynamic qualification kept live audiences aligned with current data, subscription, suppression and engagement states.

Recoverable data quality

Correctable records moved through visible remediation routes and could requalify automatically instead of remaining permanently excluded.

Scalable segmentation

Three commercial audience models combined Salesforce-informed business, geographic and organisational context without relying on manual list maintenance.

Extensible operating foundation

The initial rules and data routes created a stable base for the separate ongoing audience- governance and predictive-qualification phase.
