Fresh Start in Education

MCAE Campaign Operations, Performance and Revenue Influence

Operated Salesforce Marketing Cloud Account Engagement as a governed campaign and performance channel across segmented email, events, webinars and gated content, connecting marketing activity with engagement, purchase and revenue-influence reporting.

Marketing Automation Salesforce & CRM Data & Analytics Commercial Strategy
Project overview

Owned MCAE campaign operations across more than 50 email campaigns, activating the rebuilt audience architecture, segmentation, consent governance and list-hygiene controls to support reliable execution and measurement. Increased average open rate from approximately 8% to 40%, achieved an 84% best campaign open rate, and connected email activity with approximately £419,475 in internally modelled revenue influence.

01

From platform rebuild to active channel

Following the Mailchimp-to-MCAE migration, the organisation needed the rebuilt environment to become a dependable commercial channel rather than remain a technical implementation. Campaign activity needed to continue while new audience, consent and data-quality controls were embedded into everyday operations.

The work spanned recurring email campaigns, event promotion, webinar journeys, gated content, reporting and stakeholder communication during trading conditions affected by changes to government rules.

  • Campaign continuity after a major migration
  • Activation of newly governed audience structures
  • Several campaign and content formats
  • Commercial pressure during difficult trading periods
  • Need for measurable downstream influence

02

Single-owner campaign operations

Held sole ownership of MCAE email campaign execution across more than 50 campaigns. Work included planning, audience selection, segmentation activation, campaign construction, quality checks, scheduling, monitoring and performance analysis.

The rebuilt list architecture supplied cleaner, permission-aware audiences, while repeatable operating practices connected each campaign with Salesforce visibility and practical follow-up requirements.

  • 50+ email campaigns
  • Campaign planning and construction
  • Audience selection and activation
  • Pre-send checks and release management
  • Post-campaign analysis and reporting

03

Salesforce-informed segmentation

Managed communications and segmentation across approximately 1,000 Salesforce Accounts and 16,000 Salesforce Leads. Dynamic, static and segmentation lists translated CRM context into campaign audiences while respecting consent, suppression and list-hygiene requirements.

This connected campaign execution with relevant organisation, audience and lifecycle context instead of relying on undifferentiated bulk sends.

  • Approximately 1,000 Salesforce Accounts
  • Approximately 16,000 Salesforce Leads
  • CRM-informed audience selection
  • Governed dynamic and static lists
  • Permission-aware segmentation

04

Events, webinars and gated content

Extended MCAE activity beyond recurring email campaigns to support eight events, one gated-content campaign and two webinars. Email journeys covered invitations, promotion, registration support and relevant follow-up.

Webinar work also included live Zoom operation or hosting. This was an operational and technical responsibility rather than acting as the content presenter.

  • Email support for eight events
  • One gated-content campaign
  • Invitation and marketing for two webinars
  • Live Zoom operation or hosting
  • Campaign-linked follow-up activity

05

Performance improvement and reporting

Monitored campaign delivery and engagement, compared results, and used performance evidence to improve future campaign decisions. Average open rate increased from approximately 8% to 40%, while the strongest individual campaign achieved an 84% open rate.

Performance reporting focused on measures that could be stated consistently. A separate click measure was tracked internally, but it is not presented here because its definition as click-through rate or click-to-open rate requires clarification.

  • Average open-rate monitoring
  • Campaign comparison and analysis
  • Best campaign open rate of 84%
  • Stakeholder-facing performance reporting
  • Evidence-led campaign refinement

06

Commercial influence and stakeholder alignment

Connected campaign activity with an internal attribution model combining email-attributed and email-influenced revenue. On that basis, email activity was associated with approximately £419,475 in revenue influence. The measure represents attributed and influenced value within the internal model, not revenue claimed as solely caused by email.

Worked across IT, sales, leadership, finance and client services, primarily with one director and with occasional C-suite involvement. Reporting helped translate campaign execution into commercial evidence that different stakeholders could use.

  • Internal email-attribution support
  • Approximately £419,475 in modelled influence
  • Marketing-to-sales alignment
  • Finance and leadership reporting
  • Cross-functional campaign coordination
