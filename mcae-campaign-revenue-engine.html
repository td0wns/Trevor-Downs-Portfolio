Fresh Start in Education

Email Campaign Operations Across Mailchimp and MCAE

I ran the email channel before, during and after the move from Mailchimp to MCAE. The audience stayed the same, which made the change in performance easier to judge.

Marketing Automation Salesforce & CRM Data & Analytics Commercial Strategy
Project overview

I owned more than 50 campaigns across both platforms. Mailchimp activity was associated with about £419,475 in the 2025 internal revenue model. In MCAE, average open rate rose from 8% to 40%, the best campaign reached 84% and £127,642.98 in realised revenue was attributed between February and May 2026.

Key result Recorded approximately £127.6k in realised MCAE-attributed revenue from February to May 2026 after moving the audience from Mailchimp.

01

The platform still had to earn its keep

A successful migration is not the same as a useful marketing channel. Campaigns had to keep going while new audience, consent and data rules settled into daily operation.

The workload included regular email, event promotion, webinar journeys, gated content and reporting during trading conditions affected by changes to government rules.

  • Campaign continuity after a major migration
  • Activation of newly governed audience structures
  • Several campaign and content formats
  • Commercial pressure during difficult trading periods
  • Need for measurable downstream influence

02

Single-owner campaign operations

I was the sole owner of MCAE campaign execution for more than 50 campaigns. I planned the work, selected audiences, built and checked each send, scheduled it and reviewed the result.

The rebuilt lists supplied cleaner audiences and Salesforce held the follow-up context. Because the channel was generating material revenue, I also set the order of Pardot/MCAE work that might interrupt it.

  • 50+ email campaigns
  • Campaign planning and construction
  • Audience selection and activation
  • Pre-send checks and release management
  • Post-campaign analysis and reporting

03

Salesforce-informed segmentation

I managed communication and segmentation for about 1,000 Accounts and 16,000 Leads. Dynamic, static and segmentation lists use CRM information without bypassing consent or suppression rules.

The result was an audience chosen for a reason, not the largest list available.

  • Approximately 1,000 Salesforce Accounts
  • Approximately 16,000 Salesforce Leads
  • CRM-informed audience selection
  • Governed dynamic and static lists
  • Permission-aware segmentation

04

Events, webinars and gated content

MCAE also handled email for eight events, one gated-content campaign and two webinars. The journeys covered invitations, promotion, registration and follow-up.

I operated or hosted the live Zoom sessions. I did not present the webinar content.

  • Email support for eight events
  • One gated-content campaign
  • Invitation and marketing for two webinars
  • Live Zoom operation or hosting
  • Campaign-linked follow-up activity

05

Performance improvement and reporting

I compared campaign results and used them to change later decisions. Average open rate rose from about 8% to 40%. The strongest individual campaign reached 84%.

The 40% figure is average open rate. It is not a click-through or click-to-open rate, and I do not claim a quantified click result here.

  • Average open-rate monitoring
  • Campaign comparison and analysis
  • Best campaign open rate of 84%
  • Stakeholder-facing performance reporting
  • Evidence-led campaign refinement

06

Commercial influence and stakeholder alignment

The internal attribution model combines revenue attributed to email with revenue influenced by it. On that basis, Mailchimp activity was associated with about £419,475 during 2025. That is the modelled influence, not revenue I claim email caused on its own.

From February through May 2026, the model recorded £127,642.98 in realised revenue attributed to MCAE. “Realised” means money received into the company's accounts during that period. Additional provision value was still unrealised.

I worked with IT, sales, finance, client services and leadership, mostly through one director with occasional C-suite involvement. The reports gave each group a commercial view of the channel.

  • Internal email-attribution support
  • Approximately £419,475 in modelled Mailchimp influence during 2025
  • Marketing-to-sales alignment
  • Finance and leadership reporting
  • Cross-functional campaign coordination
