Fresh Start in Education

Commercial Systems Operating Model and Change Leadership

Connected Salesforce and MCAE platform change with clearer ownership, governed workflows and practical working practices across sales, marketing, CRM and operations.

Leadership & Stakeholder Management Operations Commercial Strategy
Project overview

Redesigned how commercial teams understood and acted on connected education-sector organisations. Established shared data structures, responsibilities, checkpoints and user guidance so platform architecture became a usable commercial operating model rather than an isolated technical implementation.

Key result Established shared operating practices across sales, marketing, CRM and operations.

01

The operating-model challenge

Schools were often treated as isolated buying entities even when they shared governance through Multi-Academy Trusts, independent school groups or other governing organisations. That limited visibility of organisational relationships, related purchases and buying momentum across connected accounts.

The challenge was broader than improving Salesforce records. Sales, marketing, CRM and operational activity needed a shared model for understanding account families and deciding how opportunities should be identified, qualified, nurtured and handed over.

02

A shared commercial model

Advocated for a relationship-led model in which schools were understood within both local-authority and private-governance structures. Identified and segmented school-account membership across 215 private governing organisations, representing approximately half of the school-account database.

The resulting architecture allowed teams to assess schools within wider account families while retaining the distinct influence of local authorities, private governing organisations and individual purchasing activity.

  • Mapped schools to their wider organisational context
  • Defined a consistent account-family view
  • Connected public and private governance signals
  • Reduced reliance on individual interpretation

03

Workflows, ownership and controls

Converted the model into repeatable Salesforce and MCAE workflows. Custom metadata, lookup keys, validation rules and guided Screen Flows aligned Accounts, Contacts and Leads with the correct governing structure while protecting data completeness.

Purchase signals from one organisation could then initiate a controlled sequence: identify related records, add appropriate prospects to Salesforce Campaigns, validate and grade them in MCAE, nurture genuine opportunities and route qualified records to sales.

  • Defined system checkpoints and safeguards
  • Created guided, context-aware user workflows
  • Connected marketing qualification with sales handoff
  • Kept validation between a signal and commercial action

04

Change leadership and adoption

Supported the move from school-level selling to an account-family approach through stakeholder communication, documentation, user guidance, training and troubleshooting. This helped teams understand not only how to use the new workflows, but why the organisational relationships mattered commercially.

Adoption was designed into the system through guided interfaces and safeguards. Users could align records, review related Accounts and Leads, correct selections and request new governing-organisation records within one controlled process.

05

From platform change to operating practice

Salesforce became the governed foundation for organisational context and guided user action, while MCAE supported validation, grading and nurture. Salesforce Campaigns connected the two, creating a visible progression from buying signal to qualified commercial follow-up.

This joined technology, data, process and people into one operating model. The platforms supported clearer team decisions and repeatable behaviours instead of remaining separate technical systems.

Outcomes

What the operating-model change enabled

Shared organisational context

Sales and marketing could understand schools within their wider account families rather than treating every record independently.

Repeatable commercial workflows

Connected related-purchase signals with validation, nurture, qualification and sales routing through governed stages.

Clearer user decisions

Guided processes, validation rules and review steps reduced manual interpretation and protected organisational data quality.

Sustained adoption

Documentation, training, troubleshooting and embedded guidance helped teams adopt the architecture as part of everyday work.
