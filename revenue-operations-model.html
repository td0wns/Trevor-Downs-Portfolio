Fresh Start in Education

Commercial Systems Operating Model and Change Leadership

I proposed a relationship-led way of working across sales, marketing, client services and operations, won senior approval and then built it into Salesforce and MCAE.

Leadership & Stakeholder Management Operations Commercial Strategy
Project overview

The model changed how commercial teams treated schools that share a MAT or another governing body. I presented it over several meetings, secured approval from the Director and CEO, then defined the data, responsibilities and checkpoints needed to use it.

Key result Secured Director and CEO approval for a relationship-led commercial operating model adopted through the formal company process.

01

The operating-model challenge

Schools were often treated as separate buyers even when they shared a MAT, independent-school group or another governing body. Teams could miss a relationship, a purchase elsewhere in the group or signs of wider buying interest.

Tidier Salesforce records would not solve that on their own. Sales, marketing and operations needed to agree what an account family meant and what should happen when one member showed interest.

02

A shared commercial model

I argued for showing each school in both its Local Authority and private-governance context. I then researched and segmented 215 private governing organisations, covering roughly half of the school-account database.

Teams can now see a school on its own and as part of a wider group, without losing the separate influence of a Local Authority or the school's own purchase history.

  • Mapped schools to their wider organisational context
  • Defined a consistent account-family view
  • Connected public and private governance signals
  • Reduced reliance on individual interpretation

03

Workflows, ownership and controls

I put the model into Salesforce and MCAE. Custom Metadata Types, lookup keys, validation rules and Screen Flows align Accounts, Contacts and Leads with the correct governing body.

A purchase can now start a defined sequence. Salesforce finds the related records and adds suitable prospects to a Campaign. MCAE checks and grades them before genuine opportunities enter nurture or reach sales.

  • Defined system checkpoints and safeguards
  • Created guided, context-aware user workflows
  • Connected marketing qualification with sales handoff
  • Kept validation between a signal and commercial action

04

Change leadership and adoption

I built the case for moving beyond school-by-school selling and presented it over several meetings. It went first to the Director, then to the CEO, before entering the formal company process.

Guided screens and safeguards made the new behaviour easier to follow. Documentation, training and troubleshooting helped it stick. The change influenced email marketing, how teams discussed related Accounts and the wider brand voice.

05

Putting the model into daily work

Salesforce holds the organisational context and guides the user's action. MCAE handles validation, grading and nurture. Salesforce Campaigns carry the signal between them so the handoff remains visible.

The important change was not the software alone. Teams had a shared interpretation of the data and a repeatable way to act on it.

Outcomes

What became different

Shared organisational context

Sales and marketing can see a school in the context of its wider account family.

Repeatable commercial workflows

A related purchase follows a known route through checks, nurture and sales handoff.

Clearer user decisions

Guided processes and validation rules reduce guesswork while protecting the underlying relationship data.

Sustained adoption

Senior approval gave the change authority; training and support made it part of everyday work.
