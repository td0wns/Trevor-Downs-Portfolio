Fresh Start in Education

Ongoing MCAE Audience Governance and Predictive Qualification

Maintains and extends a production MCAE audience system through reversible qualification, lifecycle controls, predictive buyer grading and human review where automation cannot safely resolve uncertain data.

Marketing Automation Salesforce & CRM Data & Analytics Commercial Strategy Implementation & Change
Project overview

Since February 2026, owned the continuing governance and optimisation of the MCAE audience architecture: monitoring live rules, refining segmentation and adding controls so prospect status reflects current evidence rather than permanent historic assumptions.

Key result Implemented reversible qualification, time-based score resets and human review for unresolved audience data.

01

The post-launch governance challenge

Once the initial MCAE build entered production, audience rules could not remain static. Source data, engagement, permissions and commercial priorities continued to change, while incomplete or siloed information could weaken day-to-day decisions.

Essential data-quality work had previously been inconsistent or had not happened at all. The operating model therefore needed automated guardrails, active monitoring and clear intervention routes rather than relying on repeated manual checks.

  • Changing qualification and consent states
  • Evolving Salesforce and organisational data
  • Temporary engagement signals
  • Correctable records requiring remediation
  • Exceptions that automation could not resolve safely

02

Reversible qualification and current-state scoring

Extended qualification so prospects could enter and leave profiles as their data, behaviour and time-sensitive signals changed. This prevented a prospect from remaining permanently qualified because of activity that no longer represented current buying readiness.

Added time-based score controls, dynamic profile degradation and formal sub-profiles beneath primary qualification profiles. The design separated behavioural momentum from structural fit while supporting more specific audience and journey allocation.

  • Dynamic qualification and unqualification
  • Time-based prospect score resets
  • Profile degradation as temporary signals expire
  • Formal qualification sub-profiles
  • Behavioural readiness separated from commercial fit

03

Known, inferred and unknown states

Designed explicit known, inferred and unknown states so the system did not treat uncertain information as established fact. This made the level of confidence visible within qualification and routing decisions.

Where the architecture could not resolve an unknown state, records moved into an administrator review queue. That created a deliberate human checkpoint inside an otherwise automated system and allowed promising early cases to be assessed without claiming an unsupported quantified outcome.

  • Certainty-state classification
  • Inference kept distinct from verified data
  • Administrator exception queues
  • Human review for unresolved cases
  • Safer downstream qualification decisions

04

Proof-of-life and re-engagement journeys

Operated proof-of-life and re-engagement automations that invited a deliberate response before a prospect was treated as disengaged. Journeys used relevant offers, explicit action requests and clear cessation-of-contact messaging.

The approach kept suppression distinct from remediation. Non-viable or non-contactable records remained suppressed, while incomplete but correctable records stayed visible for recovery and could requalify when their underlying data improved.

  • Proof-of-life automation
  • Win-back and re-engagement journeys
  • Clear disengagement messaging
  • Recoverable remediation routes
  • Automatic requalification after correction

05

Continuous production ownership

Continued monitoring qualifier, suppression, remediation, preference and unsubscribe rules across three commercial audiences. Maintained Salesforce-synchronised business type, Local Authority, regional and relationship context as operating requirements evolved.

Refined persona journeys using role, location, organisation type, engagement, content interest and tone. The work combined platform administration with commercial judgement: the architecture had to remain reliable while also becoming more useful for audience prioritisation and buyer qualification.

  • Live rule monitoring and refinement
  • Salesforce-informed segmentation
  • Persona-based journey development
  • Predictive buyer-profile grading
  • Feedback-led production iteration

Outcomes

What the ongoing governance phase enables

Current qualification

Scores and profiles can degrade as evidence ages, keeping present readiness distinct from historic activity.

More dependable decisions

Known, inferred and unknown states make uncertainty visible instead of allowing incomplete data to silently drive action.

Recoverable data quality

Correctable records remain available for remediation and can return to active audiences once they meet current rules.

Governed iteration

Automated controls and human exception review keep the live architecture useful as data and commercial needs change.
