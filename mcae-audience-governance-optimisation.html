Fresh Start in Education

Ongoing MCAE Audience Governance and Predictive Qualification

An audience rule should not become true forever. I maintain the live MCAE system so old signals expire, records can requalify and uncertain cases reach a person.

Marketing Automation Salesforce & CRM Data & Analytics Commercial Strategy Implementation & Change
Project overview

Since February 2026, I have monitored the production rules and refined how prospects are segmented. The aim is simple: current status should reflect current evidence, not an action that happened months ago.

Key result Implemented reversible qualification, time-based score resets and human review for unresolved audience data.

01

The post-launch governance challenge

Once MCAE went live, its source data, permissions and commercial priorities kept changing. Engagement also ages. A rule that was correct at launch could quietly become wrong later.

Earlier data-quality work had been inconsistent. I needed automatic safeguards, visible exceptions and a clear place for human review, not another recurring spreadsheet check.

  • Changing qualification and consent states
  • Evolving Salesforce and organisational data
  • Temporary engagement signals
  • Correctable records requiring remediation
  • Exceptions that automation could not resolve safely

02

Reversible qualification and current-state scoring

I made qualification reversible. Prospects enter and leave profiles as their data and behaviour change, so one old interaction cannot keep somebody qualified indefinitely.

Scores now reset after set periods and profiles can degrade when a temporary signal expires. Sub-profiles separate current behaviour from underlying commercial fit and route people into more specific journeys.

  • Dynamic qualification and unqualification
  • Time-based prospect score resets
  • Profile degradation as temporary signals expire
  • Formal qualification sub-profiles
  • Behavioural readiness separated from commercial fit

03

Known, inferred and unknown states

I introduced known, inferred and unknown states. The system can now show how confident it is instead of passing an assumption downstream as if it were verified data.

If automation cannot resolve an unknown, the record enters an administrator queue. A person can check promising cases without forcing the software to guess. Early results are encouraging, but I am not attaching a number before there is enough evidence.

  • Certainty-state classification
  • Inference kept distinct from verified data
  • Administrator exception queues
  • Human review for unresolved cases
  • Safer downstream qualification decisions

04

Proof-of-life and re-engagement journeys

Proof-of-life and re-engagement journeys ask for a deliberate response before treating somebody as disengaged. They use a relevant offer, a clear action and an honest message about contact ending if there is no interest.

Suppression and data repair remain separate. Non-contactable records stay suppressed. Incomplete but fixable records remain visible and can return when the underlying problem is corrected.

  • Proof-of-life automation
  • Win-back and re-engagement journeys
  • Clear disengagement messaging
  • Recoverable remediation routes
  • Automatic requalification after correction

05

Keeping the production rules honest

I monitor qualification, suppression, repair, preference and unsubscribe rules across three audiences. Salesforce business type, Local Authority, region and relationship data also need attention as operating requirements change.

Persona journeys use role, location, organisation type, activity, content interest and tone. The technical rule still needs a commercial reason to exist; accuracy alone does not make a segment useful.

  • Live rule monitoring and refinement
  • Salesforce-informed segmentation
  • Persona-based journey development
  • Predictive buyer-profile grading
  • Feedback-led production iteration

Outcomes

What the live system does differently

Current qualification

Scores and profiles can decline as evidence ages, separating current readiness from history.

More dependable decisions

Known, inferred and unknown states expose uncertainty before it drives a decision.

Recoverable data quality

Correctable records remain visible and can return when they meet the current rules.

Human review where it matters

Automation handles routine changes; administrators review the cases where the evidence is not clear enough.
